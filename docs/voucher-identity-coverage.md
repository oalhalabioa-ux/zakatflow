# Voucher identities

Display identities are read-only and independent of editable external references. Existing invoice and purchase sequences are preserved. Other record families have stable source-derived display codes; this does not introduce statutory or configurable sequential numbering.

Coverage in both ownership scopes where each product is supported:
- Bank/cash statements, receipts, payments, transfers, advances, refunds and reversals.
- Receivable/payable statements and their source details, opening liabilities, corporate loans, financing and employee advances; payroll deductions retain their event ID.
- Personal loans/financing/participation, individual executed movements and schedules.
- Corporate participation movements and investor statements.
- Asset opening/edit, purchase, addition, sale, depreciation, adjustment, disposal and transaction reports.
- Sales/purchase invoices, credit/debit notes, receipt edits and VAT financial statements.
- Deposits/bonds/sukuk contracts and a separate movement statement with its own PDF/CSV export.
- Personal Zakat payment ledgers and edit forms in all themes; corporate Zakat cash-flow rows identify the flow and its actual/planned status.
- Generic financial event workflow details and cash-management party statements.

Aggregate balances and forecast summaries do not represent individual vouchers. Their underlying detail rows carry the source identity. Unexecuted cash flows are explicitly called planned flows rather than posted payment vouchers. Draft forms show “Assigned on save” until there is an actual stored ID. No financial records, balances, dates, approval permissions or QA branches are rewritten.
